Cookie Policy

This page explains how Maison Calista uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function properly, remember preferences, and understand how visitors use our pages. Similar technologies — such as local storage, pixels, and session identifiers — may serve comparable purposes.

Cookies we use

Depending on how our website is configured, we may use the following categories:

  • Essential cookies — required for basic site functionality, security, navigation, and form submissions. These cannot be disabled without affecting core features.
  • Preference cookies — remember choices such as language selection or display preferences where applicable.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand traffic patterns, popular pages, and how visitors interact with the site, so we can improve the experience. These are only placed if analytics tools are enabled.
  • Communication cookies — may be set by embedded widgets such as maps or messaging tools to deliver their functionality.

Third-party services

We may use third-party tools that set their own cookies, including:

  • Embedded maps (for example, Google Maps on our contact page)
  • Analytics platforms (if enabled)
  • Form or communication widgets
  • WhatsApp or similar messaging integrations

These providers process data under their own privacy and cookie policies. We encourage you to review their documentation for further detail.

Cookie duration

Cookies may be session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (remaining on your device for a defined period or until you delete them). Duration depends on the cookie's purpose and the third-party provider's settings.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View which cookies are stored on your device
  • Block all or third-party cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Receive a warning before a cookie is placed

Blocking essential cookies may affect certain site features, including form submissions and embedded maps. Where required by applicable law, we will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies.

Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. There is no universal standard for how websites should respond. We currently do not alter our practices based on DNT signals, but we limit tracking to what is proportionate and purposeful.

Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
