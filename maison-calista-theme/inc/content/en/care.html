Care & support

Attentive care that preserves independence

A dedicated daily team provides personal support, daily living assistance, and a vibrant social life — while respecting each resident's autonomy and dignity.

Daily team

Personal support, every day

Our in-house team is present throughout the day to assist with daily living, encourage participation in activities, and ensure residents feel safe, valued, and connected. Support is warm and discreet — never clinical, never impersonal.

Personal support

Individual attention adapted to each resident's needs, preferences, and pace. We learn what matters to you — morning rituals, favourite meals, preferred activities.

Daily living assistance

Help with dressing, mobility, meals, and the small rituals that make a day feel normal. Laundry, cleaning, and bedding are included in long-term packages.

Activities & social life

A full programme of outings, workshops, and shared moments that combat isolation. Residents choose what inspires them; our team handles logistics and accompaniment.

Preserve independence

Support designed to empower — never to take over. Residents remain actors in their own lives, making choices and maintaining dignity at every stage.

Medical network

External healthcare professionals

Maison Calista works with trusted external doctors, nurses, physiotherapists, and psychologists who visit the residence or receive residents at their practices as needed. We are a boutique residence with a dedicated care team — not a medical facility — and we coordinate professional healthcare with clarity and care.

We arrange appointments, accompany residents when appropriate, and maintain clear communication with families regarding health and care plans.

  • General practitioners and specialists
  • Nursing and physiotherapy
  • Psychological support
  • Hairdresser and pedicure (included in long-term packages)
  • Hospital access in Marrakech when required

A holistic approach

Care team and external professionals, working together

Daily support at Maison Calista is delivered by our in-house team. Medical and therapeutic needs are met through our network of external professionals — ensuring residents receive the right level of care without the atmosphere of a hospital or nursing home.

In-house team

Present every day for personal support, meals, activities, housekeeping, and family communication.

External doctors

Trusted general practitioners and specialists who visit or receive residents at their Marrakech practices.

Therapists & nurses

Physiotherapy, nursing care, and psychological support coordinated by our team as individual needs evolve.

Family partnership

Regular updates, prompt responses to concerns, and transparent communication at every stage.

Family partnership

Communication and coordinated care

Families are partners in care. We provide regular updates, respond promptly to concerns, and organise external care when additional support is needed.

Whether arranging a specialist visit, adapting a care plan, or preparing for a family stay, our team ensures that medical, social, and family dimensions remain aligned. Every room has high-speed internet for video calls — so loved ones abroad stay close, every day.
